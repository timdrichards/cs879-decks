TA Scenarios

Situations to argue about in a small group · COMPSCI 879, UMass Amherst

Seven situations that come up in a teaching assistantship. They are written to be read before the session, argued about in a small group, and disagreed about honestly. Every course, instructor and student here is invented. Nothing on this page describes a real person, a real course, or a real incident at any institution.

These are free to use. If you are a TA or a UCA anywhere, in this course or not, take them and run them with your own staff. They work best with four or five people who assist different courses, because the disagreement is the point.

How to run one

Everyone reads the scenario beforehand. Pick someone to chair and someone to take notes. Spend eight minutes on the three questions at the bottom of each scenario, then have the chair report back in ninety seconds. Ask about facts before anyone gives advice.
