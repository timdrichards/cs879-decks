What this is

Your notes from observing your partner, taken while you watch or straight afterwards. Nothing here is sent anywhere. It saves in this browser, and when you are done you copy the report out and submit it to Canvas in the Friday October 30 session.

Do not write down any student's name. You are sitting in on someone else's class. Describe what people did, not who they were. "A student asked why the base case returns zero" is the level of detail that helps; a name is an education record and does not belong in your notes.

The three questions everyone answers

The third one is the one that gets wasted. A report saying everything was great is a report your partner cannot use. You are not being asked to be harsh. You are being asked to name one thing they could try next time, and why you think it would help.
