Three minutes

What does your course actually say?

You were asked to find the AI policy for the course you are TAing. Round the room, one word each.

  • Permissive
  • Restrictive
  • Silent
  • Could not find it

If it says nothing, that is a finding, not a failure to find.

Twenty minutes, in threes

Draft two emails

A student in your section emails: "Hi, quick question, am I allowed to use ChatGPT for homework 3? I don't want to get in trouble." You will answer within the hour.
  • Email one. The reply you would actually send. Real wording, not a summary.
  • Email two. To your supervising instructor, checking you got it right, and asking what they want you to say next time.

Whatever the syllabus says, your reply becomes the policy that student experiences.

Eight minutes

Then send it. Before you leave this room.
