The part worth staying for

Grade these four

Four submissions to the same short-answer question. Give each a grade out of 10 and say what you would do next.

Write it down before we talk. No conferring.

Show of hands

Who gave submission 2 the highest mark?

Who flagged submission 3 as suspicious?

Hold that thought.

Detection is a dead end

Detection tools perform poorly. So do experienced instructors.

Confident detection is where TAs get themselves and their students into trouble.

So ask the question you have power over instead.

What was this assignment measuring?

This prompt asked for a mechanism explanation plus a personal debugging story.

The mechanism half is now free to anyone. The story half is not, which is the only reason 1, 3 and 4 are distinguishable from 2 at all.

Not "can I catch it" but "does this assignment still measure what I wanted?"

The frame for the whole term

You are being handed judgment,
not rules

Every hour of this course is a decision like the one you just made. Nobody hands you the answer.

The semester is about building the judgment to make those calls and defend them.
